Spark PlanReader

Accepts simple, extended, formatted, cost and codegen modes from Spark 3.x and 4.x (AQE and Photon plans too), and event logs (JSON lines with SQL metrics). Everything stays in this browser.

See what your Spark job really does

Paste the output of df.explain(), EXPLAIN FORMATTED or a Spark event log. PlanReader draws the plan, marks stages and codegen blocks, and points out shuffles, joins that could be broadcast, skew, Python UDFs, missing partition pruning and more, with fixes you can copy.
